Norse laser files.

Vector files for laser-cutting and engraving wooden versions of the comb, the coins and the raven medallion. They are working files for testing, not a finished product, and none has been burned on a laser yet.

How to use them

  • All files are SVG, in real millimetres. Import at 100%, with no scaling.
  • Red hairline means cut through.
  • Black fill means engrave (burn). Faces come out dark on pale wood.
  • Comb: let the grain run left to right, along the teeth. The gaps between teeth are 1.55 mm, close to a laser’s cut width, so test on scrap first.
  • Medallion back: it is not mirrored. It reads correctly on the back face, so turn the piece over and engrave it as it lies. The border ring sits about 1 mm inside the cut circle, so the piece must not shift between engraving and cutting.
  • Suggested first test: burn the coin test sheet on one piece of maple or basswood scrap and check how clear the fine lettering stays.
