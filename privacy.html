Privacy

The Cryptic Bureau is a small project run from Ottawa. This page says what we collect, why, and how to have it removed. Questions or deletion requests: hello@crypticbureau.com.

What we collect

  • Feedback forms (the feedback pages and the feedback box inside the online games): what you write, an optional rating, and your email only if you choose to give it. If you tick “include my game stats”, the time, hints and tries for each puzzle are added. No puzzle answers are sent.
  • Borrow and update requests: your name, email, preferred week and any note. We use these to answer you.
  • Hint pages: a log of each run: which hints were opened and when, how long each lock took, an optional team name, your device and browser type, and the game code if you share a run. It lets teams compare their times with earlier teams.
  • Visits: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views. It uses no cookies and does not identify you.

What stays on your device

The online games save your progress, notes and light or dark setting in your own browser. We never receive that. If you use “Play together”, the shared table is kept on a server for that room only.

What we do with it

  • Your email is used only to reply to you or to send the update you asked for.
  • We don’t sell or share your details, and we run no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Feedback is read by the people making the games, to decide what to fix.

Where it is kept

On Cloudflare, which hosts this site and holds our database and email routing. A feedback alert is emailed to the Bureau’s own inbox each time a form is sent.

How long we keep it

Feedback and requests that include an email address are deleted after 12 months. Hint-page run logs are kept as statistics.

Your choices

Email hello@crypticbureau.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Give the email address you used and we will remove it. Every email field on our forms is optional except on the borrow form, where we need it to reply.
